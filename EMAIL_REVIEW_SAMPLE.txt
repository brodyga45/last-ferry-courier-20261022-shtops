LAST FERRY COURIER — EMAIL REVIEW SAMPLE
Prepared by Shtops at Brodyga45, 23 October 2026

This is a proposed launch email for our own game. It is a work sample, not a
client campaign, a sent marketing email, or evidence of conversion results.
The game uses AI-developed code, writing and vector artwork.

BRIEF
Introduce a complete, free, three-evening browser puzzle to people who enjoy
quiet, short games. The next action is to open and play the game.

SUBJECT OPTIONS
A. Last Ferry Courier is live
   Clear and factual, but the name alone gives a new reader little reason to open.

B. Three small promises. One last boat.
   Recommended for this audience: it communicates the game's stakes and tone.
   The preview text should immediately explain that it is a turn-based puzzle,
   so a reader does not mistake it for a real ferry announcement or timed offer.

C. Beat the clock before the last boat!
   I would avoid this version. It suggests a fast countdown, while the game
   spends travel minutes only when the player chooses a road.

RECOMMENDED EMAIL
Subject: Three small promises. One last boat.
Preview: A free, turn-based route puzzle. Thinking costs no time.

There are three parcels to deliver before the last ferry leaves.

Plan your route across three different evenings. An early closing gate changes
your priorities; on the last evening, delivering a lamp can open a new way home.
Each road costs travel minutes, but thinking never runs the clock.

Undo a road or restart an evening whenever you want to try a different route.

[Play Last Ferry Courier]
https://brodyga45.com/games/last-ferry-courier/

WHY THESE EDITS
- The opening gives the player one concrete task instead of a list of features.
- The time rule answers the likely concern raised by "last ferry."
- Two changed conditions show that the three evenings have different puzzles.
- One main link keeps the email focused on playing.

BEFORE LAUNCH
Confirm the link opens the intended reviewed version and the first screen
explains how to start. Check the actual email render, link and button on the
chosen email platform. Choose recipients who opted in and use the sender's
normal unsubscribe process for a real campaign.

Do not add claims about endless levels, offline installation, physical-phone
testing, player counts, enjoyment, accessibility certification or conversion
gains: we have not established those results.

There is no A/B outcome here. Subject B is an editorial recommendation to test,
not a measured winner. This sample was prepared with Codex.

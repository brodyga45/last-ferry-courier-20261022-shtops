    How to play

    Visit Market, Garden and Lighthouse in any order. Parcels are delivered automatically on your first visit. Finish at Ferry. Numbers on roads show minutes spent; you start with 14. You may revisit stops, but every trip costs time. The clock advances only when you travel. Use the buttons or the map; Tab and Enter work too.
